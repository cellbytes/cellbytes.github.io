We're excited to launch the official Cellbytes news: a space where we share updates on our work, insights from the field of digital hematopathology, and news from related, cutting-edge research.

## What We're Building

Cellbytes is a digital platform designed to bring AI-assisted analysis to hematology laboratories. Our focus is on automating the classification and quantification of blood cells from bone marrow and blood smear samples in a way that is accurate, transparent and trusted by hematopathologists. With our frontier computer vision models trained on millions of expert-labeled cells, we are able to provide consistent results that can be validated with a simple and powerful web user interface.

Behind every slide is a patient. That's why we hold ourselves to the highest standards when it comes to regulatory compliance, clinical validation, and collaboration with the medical community.
