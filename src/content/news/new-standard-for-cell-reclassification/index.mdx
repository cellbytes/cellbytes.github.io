With numerous blood cell classification tools on the market, choosing the right software comes down to two critical factors: model accuracy and daily operational efficiency.

Cellbytes was built to set a new benchmark for both.

**Trained at Gigascale** At the core of Cellbytes is a proprietary Foundation model trained with tens of millions of cells sourced from a multi-center, multi-scanner dataset. To our knowledge, it represents the largest specialized training set for cell classification available today designed explicitly to handle both clinical diversity and technical nuances of different scanning hardware and magnification levels.

**The Cell Similarity Network** Anyone who has used cell classification software knows the main bottleneck: spending hours manually clicking and dragging individual misclassified cells. Cellbytes eliminates this friction through our proprietary Cell Similarity Network, which is built on the foundation model. By automatically grouping visually and morphologically similar cells together, the platform enables you to reclassify entire clusters simultaneously. This dramatically reduces manual effort, speeds up sample sign-offs, and lets you focus on complex diagnostic decisions.
